How the Guided Workflow Works

A simple step-by-step flow for a first-time user or challenge evaluator:

1
Select Learner:

Choose a learner from the search dropdown (e.g. Alice Chen) to inspect their profile and history.

2
Review Mastery:

See skill mastery computed via EWMA, and verify that selecting an earlier time cutoff excludes future attempts without leakage.

3
Get Recommendations:

Generate ranked recommendations with multi-factor scoring (Skill Gap 45%, Difficulty Fit 35%, Relevance 20%).

4
Inspect Reasons & Prerequisite Gates:

Inspect transparent reason codes and see which activities are prerequisite-blocked until earlier skills reach ≥ 0.70.
